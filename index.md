Privacy Policy for ColorSham

Last updated: [October 8, 2026

ColorSham is a simple color puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and what choices you have.

1. Information We Store Locally

ColorSham stores game scores on your device, including your best score and the level reached for each difficulty. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2. Advertising

ColorSham uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device to provide, measure, and improve ads. This may include device identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related data, depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices.

AdMob is provided by Google. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services here:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, we may ask for your consent before showing personalized ads or using certain advertising technologies.

If you are in a US state with applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to opt out of the sale, sharing, or use of personal information for targeted advertising. Where required, ColorSham will provide privacy choices through Google’s consent and privacy messaging tools.

You can also limit ad tracking through your iOS device settings.

4. Children’s Privacy

ColorSham is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps. If the app is later marketed to children, additional child privacy protections may be required.

5. Legal Bases for Processing in the EU/UK

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, advertising-related processing may rely on consent or legitimate interests, depending on the type of data and ad use. You may withdraw consent or change your privacy choices where available in the app.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct, or object to certain uses of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent or opt out of targeted advertising.

Because ColorSham itself only stores scores locally on your device, deleting the app will remove locally stored game data. For advertising data handled by Google, please review Google’s privacy controls:
https://myadcenter.google.com/
https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or directly share your locally stored game scores. Advertising data may be processed by Google AdMob and its advertising partners as described in Google’s policies and consent messages.

8. Data Retention

Local scores remain on your device until you delete the app or clear the app’s data. Advertising-related data is handled by Google according to its own retention practices.

9. International Data Transfers

Google and its partners may process advertising-related data in countries outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards may apply.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by changing the “Last updated” date above.
